### Summary Scores Table (rows 17–27)

Column layout: `Criterion | Weight (%) | [Prototype A] | [Prototype B] | [Prototype C] | [Prototype D]`

> Agent instruction: Use actual prototype names from PM descriptions. Add/remove
> prototype columns as needed (2–4 columns). Remove unused prototype columns entirely.

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Prototype A | Prototype B | Prototype C |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Technical Feasibility | 25% | {score_1A} | {score_1B} | {score_1C} |
| UX Quality | 25% | {score_2A} | {score_2B} | {score_2C} |
| Development Cost | 20% | {score_3A} | {score_3B} | {score_3C} |
| Delivery Timeline | 15% | {score_4A} | {score_4B} | {score_4C} |
| Risk Level | 15% | {score_5A} | {score_5B} | {score_5C} |
| **Weighted Score** | — | `=SUMPRODUCT(weights, scoresA)` | `=SUMPRODUCT(weights, scoresB)` | `=SUMPRODUCT(weights, scoresC)` |
| **Rank** | — | `=RANK(WS_A, all_WS)` | `=RANK(WS_B, all_WS)` | `=RANK(WS_C, all_WS)` |

**Weighted Score formula (manual):**
`Weighted Score = Σ (Weight_i% × Score_i) / 100`
Result range: 1.00 – 5.00. Higher = better.

**Cell formatting:**
- Score cells (1–5): number, 1 decimal place
- Weighted Score: number, 2 decimal places, **bold**
- Rank: integer, **bold**
- Rank 1 row: green fill (#C6EFCE)

### Score Detail Matrix (rows 6 onward)

Column layout:
`Criterion | Weight (%) | [Proto A] Raw | [Proto A] Weighted | [Proto B] Raw | [Proto B] Weighted | ... | Notes`

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Proto A Raw | Proto A Wtd | Proto B Raw | Proto B Wtd | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| Technical Feasibility | 25% | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | `{rationale}` |
| UX Quality | 25% | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | `{rationale}` |
| Development Cost | 20% | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | `{rationale}` |
| Delivery Timeline | 15% | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | `{rationale}` |
| Risk Level | 15% | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | {1-5} | `=raw×weight/100` | `{rationale}` |
| **Total** | **100%** | — | `=SUM(wtd_A)` | — | `=SUM(wtd_B)` | — |

**Weighted column formula per cell:**
`Weighted = Raw Score × Weight% / 100`
Sum of Weighted column = Weighted Score (matches Sheet 1 Summary).

**Notes column:** agent writes a 1-sentence rationale for each score.
If data was insufficient: write "Insufficient data — neutral score (3) applied."

---

### Scoring Scale Reference (below the matrix)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Excellent / very easy / lowest cost / fastest / minimal risk |
| 4 | Good / manageable |
| 3 | Neutral / average / uncertain |
| 2 | Challenging / above average cost or risk |
| 1 | Very difficult / expensive / high risk / slowest |

> Note: All criteria use the "higher = better" convention. For cost/risk/complexity,
> a score of 5 means "low cost", "low risk", "easy to implement" respectively.
